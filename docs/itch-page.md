## Description (그대로 붙여 넣기)

```
Stamp. Shatter. Chain.

Shatter Stamp is a 100-second neon block puzzle. Every cell your stamp touches shatters the whole same-colored group connected to it — so find the one spot that brings the most crashing down.

HOW TO PLAY
• Aim with the mouse (or drag your finger). The board shows exactly what will break.
• Click (or lift your finger) to stamp. A stamp only lands if it breaks 9 or more blocks.
• Blocks fall into the gaps. When 6+ of one color link up, they break on their own — free chains!
• Break 2, 3, 4 or 5 colors at once (3+ blocks each) for ×1.5, ×2.2, ×3 or ×4 points.
• Big breaks leave CRYSTALS: row, column or cross. Break next to one and it clears the whole line.
• Earn extra seconds with 3+ color breaks and crystals.
• Chains and crystals fill the FEVER gauge. In FEVER, every stamp is a 3×3 that works anywhere for 6 seconds.
• HOLD a shape to save it for later — swap it back any time.

FEATURES
• Online leaderboard — no sign-up needed, you play as a guest. Every score is replayed and verified on the server.
• Neon synth music and sound effects, all generated in the browser
• Plays in the browser on desktop and mobile
• English / 한국어

CONTROLS
Mouse / touch — aim and stamp
Right click, Space, Shift or C — hold
Esc or P — pause

Want to keep your records with a Google account? Play on the official site: https://hermitcho.github.io/ShatterStamp-Web/

Privacy Policy: https://hermitcho.github.io/ShatterStamp-Web/privacy.html
```
